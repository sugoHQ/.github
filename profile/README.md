### What Sugo does

- **One click to start.** A classic Pomodoro is ready on first launch; make your own timers for deep work, study or workouts.
- **Fewer distractions.** Notifications are silenced while you work, the screen stays awake, and a calm background sound is there if you want it.
- **Your progress at a glance.** Daily to yearly statistics, streaks, and a note of what you worked on.
- **Always at hand.** A live countdown in the system tray or the menu bar.
- **At home on your desktop.** Made for KDE Plasma, just as good on other Linux desktops, native-feeling on the Mac.
- **Private by default.** Your data stays on your computer. No account, no tracking.
